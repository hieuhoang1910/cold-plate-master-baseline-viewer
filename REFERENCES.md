# References

Credible sources for the Cold Plate Master Baseline Viewer, mirrored in the app's
**About** tab. Compiled and DOI-checked via a multi-source research pass (verified
field-by-field against Crossref; 25/25 claims confirmed). Recent 2025–26 entries may
still receive final volume/page assignments.
